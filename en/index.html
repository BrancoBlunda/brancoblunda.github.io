How I work

What usually goes wrong in a software project, and how I avoid it.

01

I deliver in milestones. I don't disappear for two months and come back with the wrong thing.

Tests as a delivery requirement

A milestone isn't delivered unless it's covered by tests. That's why the systems keep running years later.

Everything stays in your name
