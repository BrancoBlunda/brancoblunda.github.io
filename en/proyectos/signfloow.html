The problem

Shops in this industry juggle projects, materials and quotes across spreadsheets, messages and memory. Nobody knows for sure what a job costs until it's done.

Before

Projects, materials and quotes across spreadsheets, messages and memory. Nobody knew what a job cost until it was finished.

After

A board with the real status of every project, and formula-based quotes before work starts.

What I built

  • Multi-company board with five phases, sub-boards per shop and automatic breakdown of each project into production tasks.
  • Quote engine with formulas, chapters, labor rates and overhead costs.
  • Public acceptance link that creates the project on the board once accepted.
  • Catalog with bills of materials by dimension and 91 preconfigured industry materials.
  • AI assistant with tool access to each company's data, with two providers and automatic failover.
  • Role-based access control and full isolation between companies.
  • Infrastructure as code and automated deployment with no static credentials.
