The problem

Clients, plants, reporting schedules for the regulator and calendars were handled in Excel and scattered scripts. There was no traceability and no control over who could see or change what.

Before

237 plants and 2,555 regulatory commitments in Excel and scattered scripts, with no traceability or access control.

After

Everything in one system, with role-based permissions across fifteen screens and 2,709 tests backing it.

What I built

  • Login and role-based access control, with three roles across fifteen screens, per-route permissions, read-only mode and a filtered menu.
  • Create, delete and edit clients, plants with versioned configuration history, and internal and external users.
  • Report manager with an annual schedule, processor and approver assignment, tracking and a services matrix.
  • Cross view of plants by report type, with date range filters.
  • Calendar module with Excel export in the client's exact format.
  • Integrated support view across 216 agents and a contracts screen.
  • Executable with a first-run wizard, sample data and rotating logs.
