The problem

The product worked, but the client didn't own it. The backend, store accounts and integrations were under the previous vendor's name. The Android camera was unstable and Google had a deadline to update the app.

Before

The backend, store accounts and integrations were under the previous vendor's name. The client didn't own their own product.

After

Everything migrated to the client's accounts and live in both stores, without losing a single user record.

What I built

  • Refactored the Android camera pipeline and fixed the processing that was cropping captures.
  • Full backend migration to the client's own infrastructure, with TLS.
  • Data migration between decoupled databases without changing a single real user record.
  • AI module for freely editing the recognized text, and a sign-up funnel with segmentation.
  • Reconfigured the five cloud integrations under the client's accounts.
  • Security hardening, with closed endpoints, exposed credentials removed, the previous vendor's accounts locked out, a firewall and verified backups.
  • Released on Google Play and the App Store under the client's accounts, including the transfer of the iOS listing.
