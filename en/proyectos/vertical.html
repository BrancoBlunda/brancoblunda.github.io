Vertical

A platform I created and built on my own, with conversational AI agents and a CRM for real estate agencies, on the official WhatsApp API. It qualifies leads, follows up and re-engages contacts 24 hours a day. Verified by Meta as a technology provider.

The stage-based CRM, from cold lead to closed. Each contact moves on its own based on how the conversation goes.

The inbox, where the agent chats on WhatsApp and pins what it learned at the top — intent, budget, area and, if it falls through, the reason.

The contact record, with what they shared, how many times they were followed up, how it ended and the salesperson's note.

Automatic follow-up at 20 hours, day 3 and day 6, with WhatsApp-approved templates. If they reply, it stops on its own.

Re-engagement of old contacts, with a daily cap and automatic opt-out. If WhatsApp lowers the number's quality rating, it pauses on its own.

Status on Meta, with the verified business and the access verification as a technology provider (Tech Provider), which is what allowed it to operate on other companies' WhatsApp accounts.

The problem

A real estate agency gets inquiries at all hours and replies when it can. A contact who doesn't hear back within minutes goes cold, and later nobody has time to re-engage them.

Before

Inquiries came in at all hours and got answered whenever possible. A contact who waits goes cold.

After

Replies in seconds, 24 hours a day, with the lead already qualified and logged in the CRM.

What I built

  • Conversational agent that qualifies the contact during the chat, on the official WhatsApp API.
  • The platform's own CRM, which keeps each contact's history and status.
  • Automatic follow-up and re-engagement.
  • Per-client configuration of the agent's behavior.
  • Platform infrastructure.
