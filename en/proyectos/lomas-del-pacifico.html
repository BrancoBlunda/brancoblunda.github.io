The problem

Collections on lots sold in installments were tracked in loose Excel spreadsheets. Nobody knew for sure who was up to date, how much was owed or since when, and every receipt was put together by hand.

Before

Installment collections lived in loose spreadsheets. Nobody knew for sure who was up to date.

After

One system with the payment plan, every payment with a receipt number and late fees calculated automatically.

What I built

  • Create, delete and edit clients, lots and developments, with each lot's status, whether available, sold or paid off.
  • Payment plans that generate installments on their own, monthly, bimonthly or quarterly.
  • Payment logging with unique receipt numbers, partial payments and overpayment validation.
  • PDF receipts and account statements.
  • Late-payment interest with configurable rate and grace days, plus waivers.
  • Revenue and delinquency reports, with filters and PDF export.
  • Migration command from the existing Excel spreadsheets, transactional and with error logging.
  • Automatic WhatsApp reminders for due dates and payment confirmations, with their own settings panel.
