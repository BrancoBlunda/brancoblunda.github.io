The problem

Trading crypto by hand means watching charts all day and deciding with partial information. I wanted a system that watched the market nonstop, required confirmation from several indicators before entering and protected capital on every trade.

Before

Following the market by hand, watching charts and deciding with a single indicator.

After

A system that watches, confirms signals with several factors and trades on its own, with stop loss and take profit calculated on every entry.

What I built

  • Trend-following strategy that only enters when RSI, MACD, the 200 EMA as a market regime filter and volume anomalies line up.
  • Dynamic stop loss and take profit, calculated on every trade.
  • Async core built on asyncio, where the trading logic and the web server run at the same time without blocking each other.
  • SQLite persistence to recover open positions after any restart.
  • FastAPI API and a web dashboard that shows the scan, detected signals and statistics live.
  • Binance connection through CCXT, Telegram alerts and a simulation mode that works without keys.
