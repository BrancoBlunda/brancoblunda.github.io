The problem

A restaurant knows how much it sold, but almost never how much it made. The real cost of a dish depends on sub-recipes whose ingredients change price all the time.

Before

They knew how much they sold, but not how much they made. The real cost of a dish depended on sub-recipes that kept changing price.

After

Automatic costing with nested recipes and up-to-date net profit per product.

What I built

  • Three-panel point of sale, fully keyboard-operable, with receipt printing.
  • Costing with recursive queries in PostgreSQL, which resolves recipes inside recipes.
  • Analytics dashboard with sales, average ticket, gross and net profit, comparisons, peak hours and best-selling products.
  • Inventory and purchasing. Stock is deducted with each sale, and logging a purchase records the expense and updates inventory in a single transaction.
  • Menu editor with categories, add-ons and combos.
  • Public site editor for each business, with live preview.
