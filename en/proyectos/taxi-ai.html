The problem

The dispatch center got tens of thousands of calls a month, and each one was handled by a person who listened to the address, located it and radioed a driver. The bottleneck was human and it didn't scale.

Before

Each call was handled by a person who listened to the address, located it and radioed a driver.

After

The call answers itself, understands the spoken address and dispatches. The operator just watches.

What I built

  • Node.js endpoint that receives calls and audio from Asterisk.
  • Audio transcription with Whisper and address extraction with a language model.
  • Geocoding with Google Maps and a geofence around Manizales and Villamaría.
  • Spoken response generated with TTS and converted to telephone format with FFMPEG.
  • Custom Asterisk dialplan for routing, driver lookups and direct transfer.
  • Assignment within a variable radius of 500 to 1,900 meters depending on the time of day.
  • Hang-up sensor that cancels the ride in the database automatically.
  • Automatic sign-up for first-time callers.
  • Two published Android apps, a passenger app with map tracking, driver chat and a security code, and a driver app.
